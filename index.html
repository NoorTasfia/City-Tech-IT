Who we are

A studio with the door open
and the kettle on.

We are sixteen people working out of a third-floor walk-up in Uttara — half building the work, half teaching the next round of people to do it.

City Tech started in a single rented room with one desk, one monitor, and a printer that mostly worked. Twelve years later we're still here — only now the room is bigger, the printer is newer, and the work is more careful.

We build websites, infrastructure and bespoke software for businesses who can't afford a misstep — and we teach the technicians, designers and freelancers who will build the next ones.

The two halves feed each other. Studio money funds the school. School graduates strengthen the studio. We like it that way.

How we work

Five steps, in order,
with the receipts.

No surprises. Every project moves through the same five rooms, and you'll know which room we're in.

Step 01

Listening

A two-hour conversation, no slides. We're trying to understand the business, not pitch you. You leave with a written brief either way.

Duration1–2 weeks
Step 02

Mapping

Sitemap, user journeys, success metrics. We agree on what "done" looks like before anyone draws a rectangle.

Duration1 week
Step 03

Drawing

Design in the open. Weekly walk-throughs, hand-marked PDFs, and zero "big reveals." If you hate it, we hear it early.

Duration3–5 weeks
Step 04

Building

Hand-coded, staged weekly, reviewed against the brief. Performance budgets agreed up front — and stuck to.

Duration6–12 weeks
Step 05

Keeping

We don't disappear at launch. A standing monthly retainer keeps the work running — and us reachable when you need us.

DurationIndefinite
